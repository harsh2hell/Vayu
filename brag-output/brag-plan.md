# Brag Plan: VAYU (AI Cyclone Intelligence)

## What is this app?
VAYU (वायु) is an automated multi-source satellite intelligence and spatiotemporal AI cyclone trajectory prediction platform engineered for the Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) and IMD, delivering sub-100ms neural forecasts with 25-pass Monte Carlo epistemic uncertainty quantification.

## The angle
Traditional numerical physics models (WRF, ECMWF) take 3 to 6 hours to compute cyclone tracks while coastal populations wait in peril. VAYU executes the full pipeline — INSAT-3D center localization, Dvorak structural classification, and 72-hour autoregressive trajectory projection — in just 95.5 milliseconds.
